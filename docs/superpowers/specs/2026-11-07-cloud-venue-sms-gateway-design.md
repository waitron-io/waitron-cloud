## Two separate uses

Security codes are free, subject only to hourly/daily abuse limits. Proposed detail: carry this policy through levels 2 and 3 and trials. No monthly security allowance or overall spending quota applies. The owner must settle recipient, resend, box-installation and account limits; purchases cannot raise them. Booking use must not consume, reserve or block security capacity. The supplier arrangement has not yet been checked and accepted for this requirement.

Booking confirmations, changes, cancellations and reminders require an **extra paid allowance**, included in no level by default. Propose offering it at level 1 and during trials, without another subscription. Billing must separately support its charge and invoice. Amount, price, one-off versus recurring payment, renewal/reset and refunds remain owner choices; no prepaid balance or monthly period is chosen. You authorise the purchase before sending. Exhaustion stops sending, without automatic charges, alternative delivery or reminders saved for later.

Suggested Waitron wording, subject to its agreement: when security sends reach their limit, show “Code not sent. Try again at {time}” / “Código no enviado. Inténtalo de nuevo a las {hora}”. Offer another usable sign-in proof only if already enrolled; require no new alternative enrolment. Never weaken required proof or count email alone as a second factor. SMS cannot replace stronger enrolled proof; this introduces no identity methods. For bookings distinguish “Booking SMS not sent: no paid allowance” / “SMS de reserva no enviado: sin saldo de pago” from “Booking SMS not sent: allowance exhausted” / “SMS de reserva no enviado: saldo agotado”. Invent no reset date.

## Sender and message bounds

[Spain sender registration](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/sms-voice/latest/userguide/registrations-spain.html) covers the name shown to recipients. It requires brand registration in CNMC's National Alias Registry, provider-specific onboarding and qualifying certificate/representative credentials. Company/brand eligibility is not assumed. Exact casing matters; generic names are prohibited; unregistered names have been blocked since 15 September 2026. Propose a shared Waitron brand. Only if AWS is chosen: obtain its provider-details form, complete CNMC registration/verification, then submit to AWS.

[Encoding limits](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/sms-voice/latest/userguide/sms-limitations-character.html) determine how text splits into charged parts. GSM7 is a limited alphabet: usually 160 characters fit one part, 153 each when split; extension characters count twice. Other text fits 70 units, 67 each when split; some symbols use two units. Propose fixed GSM7 English/Spanish templates, checked for the largest encoded size of permitted inserted values. Reject over one part to bound message cost, not impose a security quota. Spanish é/ñ fit GSM7; á/í/ó/ú do not. For example, use “Tu clave de Waitron es {code}” and “Reserva {date}, {time}”. Omit guest names or reject incompatible values; never silently strip accents or truncate.

Provider choice comes first. Supplier/privacy, retention, supported countries, sender eligibility, numeric abuse bounds, billing commercial values and Waitron integration remain open.
