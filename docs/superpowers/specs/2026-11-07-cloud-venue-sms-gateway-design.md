# D610: Venue SMS gateway

**Design only, 2026-11-07. No build or landing authority.** Cloud would send texts for the venue's Waitron box, the computer running its point-of-sale app. **Owner-decided:** free sign-in/security texts at free level 1, subject only to hourly/daily abuse limits; booking texts require extra paid allowance, included in no level by default. Booking use must not consume, reserve or block security capacity. **Proposed:** require purchase authorisation before sending, then stop booking messages at exhaustion without automatic charges, other delivery or queued reminders. The rest of this design still needs owner review, including paid-plan/trial availability, Cloud-owner codes, prices, paid quantities and terms, providers, abuse limits, privacy/retention, initial countries, sender brand/eligibility and message-size limits.

WATCHER 2026-11-07 22:47 quotes the owner: “sign in SMS would be level 1, and bookings would be an extra paid allowance.” The watcher’s full direction at that timestamp: “sign-in and security codes are included on level 1 (free), with only an abuse limit per hour/day and never shared with any other use; booking messages to guests are an extra, paid allowance, not included in any level by default.” The [billing allowance policy](2026-10-29-cloud-billing-design.md#3-allowances-owner) links this revised SMS policy.

## Two separate uses

Propose extending the free policy through paid plans (levels 2 and 3) and trials. No monthly security allowance or overall spending quota applies. The owner must settle recipient, resend, box-installation and account limits; purchases cannot raise them. Booking use must not consume, reserve or block security capacity. The provider arrangement remains unchecked for this requirement.

Booking confirmations, changes, cancellations and reminders require an **extra paid allowance**, included in no level by default. Propose offering it at level 1 and during trials, without another subscription. Billing must separately support its charge and invoice. Amount, price, one-off versus recurring payment, renewal/reset and refunds remain owner choices; no prepaid balance or monthly period is chosen. You must authorise the purchase before sending. Stop booking messages at exhaustion, without automatic charges, alternative delivery or reminders saved for later.

Suggested Waitron wording, subject to its agreement: when security sends reach their limit, show “Code not sent. Try again at {time}” / “Código no enviado. Inténtalo de nuevo a las {hora}”. Offer another usable sign-in proof only if already enrolled; require no new alternative enrolment. Never weaken required proof or count email alone as a second factor. SMS cannot replace stronger enrolled proof; this introduces no identity methods. For bookings distinguish “Booking SMS not sent: no paid allowance” / “SMS de reserva no enviado: sin saldo de pago” from “Booking SMS not sent: allowance exhausted” / “SMS de reserva no enviado: saldo agotado”. Invent no reset date.

## Ownership and sending

Codes for venue staff/owners and booking confirmations, changes, cancellations and reminders remain proposed. The source reading at Waitron `9086f8d54eb5b231de3d0949647a80f2b144333f`, not runtime verification, found no SMS sender/caller: [bookings](https://github.com/clintongormley/waitron/blob/9086f8d54eb5b231de3d0949647a80f2b144333f/packages/bookings/src/bookings.ts#L66) store `contactPhone`; [account email](https://github.com/clintongormley/waitron/blob/9086f8d54eb5b231de3d0949647a80f2b144333f/apps/server/src/account-email.ts#L48) covers invitations, resets and email changes. Waitron still needs code to request and track these messages. Leave its existing read of the old location field unchanged.

By analogy with the [email ownership split](../../documentation-ownership.md), propose Cloud owns SMS delivery and allowance rules; Waitron manages venue codes, proof of control of enrolled numbers, validity and recovery. Propose the same free policy for Cloud account-owner codes, issued by Cloud's account service; a box cannot issue them.

Reuse [email relay](../../transactional-email-relay-contract.md#authority-belongs-to-an-installation-and-a-consenting-account) authority rules (who may send for which box, consenting account and venue, and for how long), encrypted queue and retry patterns, not its allowance policy; its [allowed message types](../../../packages/registration/src/relay-payload.ts#L9) exclude SMS. Authenticate the paired box and derive account, venue and country. Accept one international-format phone number, permitted purpose, fixed template with length limits, English/Spanish language, expiry and a message ID retained across retries. Reject arbitrary text, sender, country overrides, credentials and marketing. Require recorded guest booking consent; a stored number proves neither consent nor control. Recheck authority and consent after waits and before sending. Record no raw phone, code or body in logs.

When accepting booking messages for sending, reserve every encoded part against the separate paid allowance in one indivisible step. Refusing admission costs zero. Code attempts use hourly/daily abuse limits. Exact retries retain their original booking-use accounting and return saved status without extra charge; changed fields are refused. If provider acceptance is unknown, retain the submission and reservation; release only on proven non-submission, never twice. Never duplicate/resend uncertain work or send expired codes/reminders. Provider acceptance does not prove delivery; sends already underway cannot be recalled.

Use **one small send function** taking the checked, length-limited template, phone, expiry and stable message ID, returning accepted/rejected/unknown plus status. Add no routing service or interchangeable-provider framework. Existing IDs stay tied to their provider; switching providers must not duplicate an uncertain submission.

## Sender and message bounds

[Spain sender registration](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/sms-voice/latest/userguide/registrations-spain.html) requires CNMC brand registration, provider-specific onboarding and qualifying certificate/representative credentials. A sender alias is the displayed sender name. Company/brand eligibility is not assumed. Exact casing matters; generic aliases are prohibited; unregistered aliases have been blocked since 15 September 2026. Propose a shared Waitron brand. Only if AWS is chosen: obtain its provider-details form, complete CNMC registration/verification, then submit to AWS.

[Encoding limits](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/sms-voice/latest/userguide/sms-limitations-character.html) determine how text splits into charged parts. GSM7 is a limited alphabet: usually 160 characters fit one part, 153 each when split; extension characters count twice. Other text fits 70 units, 67 each when split; some symbols use two units. Propose fixed GSM7 English/Spanish templates, checked for the largest encoded size of permitted inserted values. Reject over one part to bound message cost, not impose a security quota. Spanish é/ñ fit GSM7; á/í/ó/ú do not. For example, use “Tu clave de Waitron es {code}” and “Reserva {date}, {time}”. Omit guest names or reject incompatible values; never silently strip accents or truncate.

Provider choice comes first.
