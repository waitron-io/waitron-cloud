# D610: Venue SMS gateway

**Design only, revised 8 November 2026.** You can choose a text instead of an email code when sign-in needs a second check, or confirm a phone number you add. SMS in Waitron needs a venue connected to Cloud. The owner chose GatewayAPI and monthly figures for every level: Registered (level 1, free) includes 10 texts per Cloud account; Connected (level 2) includes 25; Hosted (level 3) includes Connected. These figures are stated and counted, but initially unenforced. Requests must wait 60 seconds per phone number and purpose, across accounts; five wrong codes end a sign-in attempt. Nothing is built. Choosing GatewayAPI is not approval to open a provider account, spend or build.

Owner decisions are recorded by the watcher on 8 November: GatewayAPI at 10:55 (“Gateway sounds good”), fixed Cloud templates at 10:58, unenforced monthly figures at 11:11, settled sign-in rules at 11:25 (“email before SMS, it costs less”) and corrected level names at 11:39. “Count every send per account” and “make the limit a setting that can be switched on and raised” are the watcher's 11:11 directions. The watcher approved this documentation revision and the proposed 60-second/five-guess controls at 11:35. Provider enablement and implementation remain separate decisions.

## Codes and monthly figures

SMS carries codes only: phone confirmation and a second check the person chooses. Email is the default second-check delivery; SMS cannot replace an enrolled authenticator app. The separate D608 owner-lifecycle design is being revised to the settled sign-in and proof-again rules. This gateway does not decide recovery or make an SMS code sufficient on its own to sign in.

Count every send per account, including phone confirmation and uncertain provider replies. Refusing before submission counts zero; an unchanged retry keeps its original message ID and count. A text is one encoded message part; templates must fit one part. Monthly enforcement starts off, with a configurable limit that can later be enabled or raised. The [billing design](2026-10-29-cloud-billing-design.md#3-allowances-owner) makes this an exception to per-venue allowances and refusal at a monthly limit.

The 11:11 direction replaces the watcher's 7 November 22:47 hourly/daily-limit wording. Until monthly enforcement is switched on, only the request wait restrains sending: up to 1,440 texts per phone and purpose each day, with no cap on how many phones an account can text. At the earlier survey's assumed €0.0342 per part, that is about €49 per day for one phone and purpose; 10 parts cost €0.342 and 25 cost €0.855, before extras/tax. These are arithmetic examples, not a current tariff or spending cap; the [public pricing page](https://gatewayapi.com/pricing/) did not expose the Spain rate in its text read on 8 November. Confirm the actual route price before enablement.

## Requesting a code

Reuse the [email relay's authority rules](../../transactional-email-relay-contract.md#authority-belongs-to-an-installation-and-a-consenting-account): authenticate the paired box and derive its consenting account, venue and country. Cloud's account service issues Cloud-owner codes; a box cannot issue them. Waitron owns the validity of its code and checking the reply, while Cloud owns sending and account counting. Keep customer records and credentials in-country.

The caller supplies one international-format phone number, allowed purpose, template name, values, English/Spanish language, expiry and a message ID retained across retries. Cloud holds the fixed templates. Refuse free text and any value outside its template's allowed type and length: a code is digits; a time is a time. Refuse caller-selected senders, country overrides, credentials, marketing and expired requests. A stored phone number proves neither consent nor control. Recheck current authority and consent after waits and before sending. Keep raw phones, codes and bodies out of logs.

The sending service enforces 60 seconds between requests for each phone number and purpose, regardless of account or browser session. The service checking codes enforces five wrong guesses per sign-in attempt; resending does not restart that attempt or its guess count. Codes expire and work once. Suggested wait message: “Code not sent. Try again at {time}” / “Código no enviado. Inténtalo de nuevo a las {hora}”. Offer another permitted proof without weakening the account's enrolled proof requirements.

Use one small send function returning accepted, rejected or unknown with its status. If the provider's answer is unknown, the message is counted and not resent, and the person can ask for a new code after the wait. Never send expired codes. Provider acceptance does not establish delivery, and a send already underway cannot be recalled. Changing provider must not duplicate an uncertain submission.

## Message bounds and what remains open

Keep each template within one encoded part for the largest permitted values. GatewayAPI's [encoding limits](https://gatewayapi.com/docs/limitations/) explain why the alphabet matters: GSM-7 fits 160 basic characters; other characters can reduce that to 70, and some characters use extra space. Its [special-character example](https://gatewayapi.com/help-center/special-characters/) shows that “á” changes encoding. Use fixed English/Spanish templates such as “Your Waitron code is {code}” and “Tu clave de Waitron es {code}”, with bounded digits; reject incompatible or oversized values rather than stripping accents or truncating text. These public pages were read on 8 November; real provider delivery remains untested.

Start with Spanish recipients. Qualify other destination countries separately; the venue's country does not establish a guest's destination country. Sender-brand eligibility and registration, provider privacy and processing location, final templates/encoding and delivery remain unqualified before enablement. No sender, provider account, registration or send is authorised by this design.

Booking messages are outside this revision. SMS reminders are off by default; any future booking SMS offer needs the guest's opt-in. No paid-booking allowance, WhatsApp integration, automatic alternative delivery or queue of old reminders is designed here. Waitron and Cloud integration remains to build after approval.
