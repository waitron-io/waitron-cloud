# One Cloud owner: handover and lost access (D608)

**Design only; nothing built.** Each customer organisation has one Cloud account owner. To hand yours over, you name the recipient; they verify their address, enrol their own sign-in proof and accept. Your access to that organisation ends at acceptance. Your strongest enrolled proof governs sign-in, password reset and sensitive actions. If you lose all enrolled proofs and recovery codes, the proposed recovery route needs both your verified-mailbox reset link and approval by a local administrator on a currently paired venue box. The watcher accepted that combination for the campaign at 21:54; the owner may overrule. It restores your same account, including other organisations. Cloud staff cannot override recovery. Without the mailbox or a usable paired-box administrator, recovery remains unavailable; total loss is unresolved.

Details labelled **Proposed** still need owner acceptance. No implementation or landing is authorised.

If the owner has set up an authenticator app or a passkey, SMS alone does not complete a password reset (the app, a passkey or a recovery code does); SMS may still be offered for sign-in only if you show why that is safe, otherwise not. ## Your email and strongest factor

A factor is an enrolled sign-in proof: an authenticator app, SMS or a passkey with a user check on your device. Without one, your verified email is your entire identity, as at signup; there is no personal/business identity to investigate. You must enrol a factor before connecting a venue box, enabling remote access or adding a payment method. This applies to alternate routes, completion after waits and the next such action for existing accounts.

With an app/passkey enrolled, sign in with password plus an app code or saved recovery code, or a verified passkey. Password changes require current strong proof; reset requires your verified-mailbox link plus app, passkey or recovery code. A passkey needs no extra app. Exclude weaker SMS sign-in when an app/passkey exists: a stolen password and control of your phone number would otherwise expose account data. No safe weaker route has been demonstrated.

With SMS as your sole factor, sign-in requires password plus SMS; reset requires the mailbox link plus a code to your already enrolled number. **Proposed:** a saved recovery code can replace that SMS proof. SMS alone never resets a password. Strongest-factor checks also govern handover, connecting a box, enabling remote access, adding a payment method and factor/phone changes. Mailbox-only, password-only and old SMS sessions cannot weaken protection. Ordinary password reset preserves factors.

Source reading in this branch, not runtime verification: [password login](../../../packages/registration/src/password-login.ts#L95) checks only the app, then issues its challenge. Without an app, even a passkey-only account currently gets a signed-in session from its password alone; [recovery-code handling](../../../packages/registration/src/password-login.ts#L41) consumes codes tied to the app. [Reset completion](../../../packages/registration/src/password-recovery.ts#L53) checks no factor; [its completion effects](../../../packages/registration/src/password-recovery.ts#L76) preserve factors and end sessions. Password login and reset must recognise passkeys and the strongest-factor rule. [Passkeys](../../../packages/registration/src/passkeys.ts#L170) already require user verification and [create a session](../../../packages/registration/src/passkeys.ts#L179). **Proposed:** saved single-use recovery codes for every factor type, including SMS/passkey-only enrolment; changing enrolled factors replaces old codes. These capabilities are new.

## Recover the same owner

First use an available factor or, as proposed above, a saved code. If all factors/codes are lost, an administrator signed in locally on your venue's currently paired box may approve recovery. That venue must currently belong to an organisation your recovering account owns. Approval must establish actual local administrator authority and tie together the current pairing, exact Cloud account, venue, country and reset request. It expires and works once. Recheck ownership and those relationships at completion; email/browser claims cannot approve recovery.

**Proposed:** box approval replaces only the missing factor proof; the verified-mailbox reset link remains required. Watcher 2026-11-07 21:54 accepted this combination for the campaign, subject to the owner's right to overrule. It prevents venue staff with box access alone taking your account. This exceptional recovery invalidates lost factors and sessions, requires a new factor before account access and notifies your verified address. It restores the same account, including other organisations: one venue's box plus that account's mailbox can recover all of them. It never assigns a different owner.

Cloud checks the reset and its exact account/venue relationships; Waitron supplies local administrator approval. Integration is unbuilt. Without a usable paired box/administrator or the mailbox, recovery is unavailable; total loss remains unresolved. There is no Cloud staff override or identity service.

## Hand over one organisation

You sign in with your strongest factor and name the recipient's email. They verify that address, sign in, enrol their own factor and accept the exact transfer. One database transaction replaces the sole owner and ends your organisation access together, at acceptance. You give no later confirmation. First-time recipients need signup for a country account alone; [current signup](../../../packages/registration/src/signup.ts#L71) creates an organisation/business too.

**Proposed defaults:** one pending transfer per organisation, displayed 24-hour expiry and cancellation by either participant. Pending, cancelled or expired transfers leave you owner and give the recipient no access. Changing recipient requires cancellation. Security changes cancel affected pending transfers, except the recipient's required initial enrolment, which ties their fresh proof to the transfer before acceptance. Completion rechecks exact parties, that ownership has not changed (its recorded version), country, authentication and expiry after waits; competing acceptance/cancellation has one outcome. A lost reply resumes that outcome; private results require current authority.

The [country ownership plan](../plans/2026-10-22-cloud-global-country-split.md#3-single-owner-authority-and-future-handover) requires immediate revocation of former-owner sessions, approvals, support grants, consent and queued work. Consent needs independent renewal; replies must avoid revealing account existence, with attempt limits and notifications. Active recovery/replacement must reach a known outcome first. Unrelated organisations and independently authorised machine services remain. Taxpayer/legal-business identity stays unchanged; a different taxpayer needs separate Waitron handling. Cloud ownership grants no Waitron staff, support, Cloud staff operator, fiscal or activation authority; each retains its separate permission checks.

## SMS beside the app

SMS is weaker: SIM swapping can steal control of your number ([NCSC](https://www.ncsc.gov.uk/guidance/protecting-sms-messages-used-in-critical-business-processes)). **Proposed:** short-lived single-use codes, shared limits on attempts/resends, and Cloud account-security SMS separate from venue messaging allowances so exhaustion cannot block sign-in. This is no unlimited-send promise.

Public sources checked 2026-11-07: [SES](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/ses/latest/dg/Welcome.html) “is an email platform”; Pinpoint SMS/MMS/voice “are now called AWS End User Messaging” ([AWS](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/sms-voice/latest/userguide/what-is-sms-mms.html)). AWS's [pricing page](https://aws.amazon.com/end-user-messaging/pricing/) and [CSV](https://d1.awsstatic.com/onedam/marketing-channels/website/aws/en_US/business-applications/approved/documents/End-User-Messaging-SMS-Prices.ebc340b4d416d90832dd59629c4792b0deb6f8bc.csv) list Spain at US$0.07 per outbound SMS segment, a separately charged message part. One-segment code: US$0.07; retries/longer messages add cost. This is SMS delivery, not a separate service verifying one-time codes.

For branded +34 messages, [AWS](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/sms-voice/latest/userguide/registrations-spain.html) says “All alphanumeric sender IDs” “must be registered”.
