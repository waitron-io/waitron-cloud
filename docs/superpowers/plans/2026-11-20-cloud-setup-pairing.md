# Register a new box from Waitron

From the box, sign in or sign up and let Cloud connect it without a pairing code, business picker, Approve or second Connect click. During setup, this starts after the local administrator exists, before the venue form. Address lookup is available while the form is open. On save, Cloud files a fresh venue and makes Cloud email available at the email step. Skip keeps manual addresses and your own email server. You can connect later from the box using the saved venue details. These are the owner's 20 November 13:11, 13:20, 13:49 and 13:54 decisions in Lane K's questions. Lane D owns the Waitron client and shared signing contract. This supersedes the [link-only plan](2026-11-19-cloud-one-click-pairing.md).
