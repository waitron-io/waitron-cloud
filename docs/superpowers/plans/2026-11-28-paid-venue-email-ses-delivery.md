# Paid venue email through SES · slice 2c

An operator can verify one synthetic Connected or Hosted venue and send its box-signed staff mail through the SES adapter to a loopback TLS server. Fresh verification selects the venue From. Missing, lost or expired verification selects Waitron. Reply-To follows the venue’s settings. Feedback follows the original send attempt after a sender switch.
