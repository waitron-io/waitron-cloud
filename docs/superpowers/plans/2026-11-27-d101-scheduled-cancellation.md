# Schedule the test backup exit at the Connected trial end

In Billing, an allowlisted synthetic venue owner can confirm **Schedule end of test backups**. Billing shows when new snapshots stop and when the verified archive, key and manifest stop being downloadable. You can reopen Billing to see both saved dates. The Lane G watcher chose this test slice on 27 November at 12:56 and 12:59. The billing design sends an unpaid trial down the same exit path. [Billing design §8](../specs/2026-10-29-cloud-billing-design.md#8-cancelling-lapsing-and-closing-an-account)

## Record the two dates

- Add `POST /api/account/billing/schedule-test-backup-end` in `packages/account-http/src/{app,billing-account}.ts`. Accept the venue ID and the trial end date shown at confirmation. Require test venue levels, configured exit downloads, the existing Billing allowlist, a verified owner and fresh connection proof with an enrolled factor. Reuse Billing’s session, origin, request-forgery and rate checks. [Billing admission](../../../packages/account-http/src/billing-account.ts#L72) · [Connection proof](../../../packages/registration/src/connection-proof.ts#L11)
- In one transaction, lock the account and session through `requireConnectionProof`, then verified credentials, scoped owner membership, organisation, venue for update and the journal. Recheck that the venue still belongs to that organisation and business and is a test venue. For a first confirmation, read only the scoped active Connected trial against the database’s current UTC day. Refuse an expired trial or a changed preview. Set the cutoff at midnight UTC on the trial’s stored end date, the first day outside the trial. Keep `LifecycleStore.request` as a wrapper and add a caller-transaction variant in `packages/registration/src/data-lifecycle.ts`. Recheck proof after lock waits. After the first write, require the database clock to be earlier than cutoff before committing. [Journal write](../../../packages/registration/src/data-lifecycle.ts#L78)
- Under the journal lock, look up this action’s fixed operation ID for the venue first. An authorised repeat of the same date returns the stored dates even after the trial ends. Refuse a different date. First writes use the current revision and record `cancel` with a fingerprint of the confirmed date. Require no prior journal row, or an active row with no end date, cancellation or deletion. The journal enters `exit preparing` now and fixes the download deadline 2,160 hours after cutoff. The write returns only the selected venue’s dates. Billing reads the preview or the saved dates separately. [Journal key](../../../packages/registration/migrations/047_data_lifecycle.sql#L11)
- In `apps/portal/src/{api,app,billing-controller,billing-screen,billing-types,locale}.ts`, reuse the verification prompt. Ask for confirmation after proof and show both times in UTC in English and Spanish. Discard replies after the account, venue or page changes. Refresh Billing after an uncertain reply.
