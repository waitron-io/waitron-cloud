# D604 Connected trial price plan

On a local Cloud, a synthetic owner sees Connected’s monthly price including VAT, chooses the existing 30-day trial, saves a SumUp sandbox card and reads when billing is due. The watcher narrowed this slice on 18 November at 19:49. It follows [the trial and renewal design](../specs/2026-10-29-cloud-billing-design.md) and [billing plan row F](2026-10-29-cloud-billing.md#the-slices-in-order). Trial conversion, extensions and collection belong to row G.
