## Venue name dependency

The signed Waitron request sends the venue ID and environment (`packages/contract/src/signing/pairing-proof.ts:3–14` and `apps/server/src/cloud-client.ts:367–378` in Waitron). The Cloud reply carries organisation and business names (`packages/registration/src/pairing.ts:16–19,34–41`). To show the box's venue name, Waitron needs to supply it through an agreed pairing change that supports current boxes. The watcher needs to route that dependency and decide whether to clear the Cloud fixes first. The proposed interim screen shows **New venue** for a first pairing or a labelled venue ID for a returning box. A business or organisation name cannot serve as the venue name.
