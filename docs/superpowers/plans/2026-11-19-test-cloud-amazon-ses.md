# Check the Stockholm SES sandbox for the test Cloud

You need real email for workflow testing. On 16 November the owner asked to “allow turning on the services like remote access, email, etc so we can work on the workflows” and answered “1. amazon 2. 2”, choosing Amazon and real email through its Simple Email Service (SES). The campaign desk record is `20261116-2048-K-test-cloud-for-workflows.md`. The watcher put D102 next on 19 November at 12:58 and held the live Juniper box at 14:00. The [parent plan](2026-11-16-test-cloud-aws.md) governs access and spending. Claude reviewed the plan and recommended the smaller first step below. Watcher clearance is pending.

1. **Get three facts from the owner.** Ask for the existing Stockholm account’s sending status, including whether it is in the sandbox, whether it has a verified sender and valid DKIM signing, and whether any D102 feedback resources exist. Keep account details, sender addresses and exports private. The [test machine’s role](../../test-cloud-amazon-access.md) covers its EC2 access. Any later SES or feedback permission gets its own review.
2. **Bring one decision to the owner.** From that readback, list the missing configuration set, private topic and subscription, encrypted queue and dead-letter queue (for messages that could not be processed), key, and separate sending, consumer, health and administrative identities. Use the [offline policy bundle](../../ses-feedback-qualification.md#check-the-proposed-document-bundle) as the expected shape. Include the proposed scoped access, credentials, sender and any DNS changes, the verified recipient, consent and privacy boundaries, and the expected Stockholm cost. Ask which [qualification checks](2026-11-02-ses-feedback-implementation.md#enablement-checklist-separate-owner-only-provider-qualification) need evidence before the synthetic sandbox trial. Keep the full checklist as the production gate. The result of this slice is a single owner decision with actual or missing prerequisites named.
3. **Use the decisions to plan the send.** After separate authority and qualification, reuse the existing TLS mailer, policy validator and private feedback consumer. Check simulator delivery, bounce and complaint and the Cloud refusal of a subsequent handoff to a blocked recipient. For the first inbox receipt, propose one synthetic Cloud-account invitation to an approved verified mailbox in a separate startup plan. Inspect authentication and tracking in its received source, then restore Mailpit and check the local inbox. The box invitation still needs the paired grant and the Juniper trial. Any request to leave the SES sandbox goes to the owner. [AWS sandbox rules](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/ses/latest/dg/request-production-access.html) and [AWS mailbox simulator](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/ses/latest/dg/send-an-email-from-console.html).

**Files and checks.** This slice adds this one-page Markdown plan and a private watcher note. Code and test changes are zero. Check the saved Markdown with `node scripts/ci-docs.mjs`. During the later startup slice, use `packages/registration/test/ses-smtp-configuration.test.ts` and `ses-feedback-cli.test.ts` for the configuration gates, `ses-smtp-settlement.test.ts` for the committed attempt and acceptance receipt, and `mail-handoff-suppression.test.ts` for blocking. Update `docs/backlog.md`, `docs/ses-feedback-qualification.md` and `docs/test-cloud-service-controls.md` when a change proves new behaviour.
