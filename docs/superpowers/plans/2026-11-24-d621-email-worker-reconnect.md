# D621 email delivery after a database connection drops

A surviving worker should send fresh sign-in mail after it reconnects to PostgreSQL. In the free Kind rehearsal both workers exited on idle connection loss. The first fresh message spent 31.234 seconds queued after the database could take writes, including 26.621 seconds with both workers down. The other 4.613 seconds covered failed startup checks still in progress and the final startup and send. Kubernetes reported 10- and 20-second restart delays. The earlier 49.716-second delivery lacks precise restart times. The earlier 76.450-second delivery followed deliberate submission at 75 seconds and took 0.271 seconds from queue to send. The experiment cannot show whether Stockholm's workers exited, hung or stopped polling.

Leave `--once`, `--status`, claims, the 60-second lease, mail retry rules, probes, the test-Cloud worker, monitor and usage out of this slice.
